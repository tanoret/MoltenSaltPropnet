# # Optimizers which we could try out
AdamW
RAdam (Rectified Adam): More stable in early training.
Lookahead + AdamW: Combines fast convergence with stability.
Cosine Annealing: Smooth decay, good for generalization.
OneCycleLR: Boosts learning rate early, then decays — often improves convergence.
